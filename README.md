## CoolHomePatch

Do you love the mods CoolHome and SafehouseCustomization+ but also love freezing to death in your own home? Then this mod is **NOT** for you. 

This mod *mostly* patches a small incompatibility between them by detecting and assigning a GUID to Fire objects at runtime. You will still see a short burst of errors in the console when entering a new scene before my mod runs and fixes them. Other than that, it should allow these two awesome mods to coexist. Please let me know if you notice any errors when it comes to your cold home warming up!
